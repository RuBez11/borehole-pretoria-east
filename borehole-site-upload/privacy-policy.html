Privacy & POPIA Notice

Last updated: 2026

Borehole PTA East ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 ("POPIA"). This notice explains what information we collect through this website, why we collect it, and how it is used and shared.

What we collect

When you submit a quote request, contact form, or WhatsApp/call enquiry through this site, we may collect: your full name, phone number, suburb/address, email address, and any details you provide about the service you need (e.g. borehole drilling, repair, yield testing).

Why we collect it

We collect this information to: respond to your enquiry, provide you with an accurate quote, arrange a site assessment, and connect you with a qualified drilling contractor able to service your area. We do not collect information for any purpose beyond fulfilling your service request.

Who we share it with

This website operates as a lead-referral service for local borehole drilling contractors in Pretoria East. This means that, with your consent, your contact details and enquiry information may be shared with one or more of our contracted drilling partners so that they can contact you directly to provide a quote or carry out the requested work. We only share information with partners for this specific purpose — we do not sell or rent your information to third parties for marketing or unrelated purposes.

Legal basis for processing

We process your information based on your explicit consent, given when you tick the consent checkbox on our forms, or when you voluntarily contact us via phone, WhatsApp, or email with a service request.

How long we keep it

We retain enquiry information only for as long as necessary to fulfil your request and for a reasonable follow-up period afterward (typically up to 12 months), unless you ask us to delete it sooner or an ongoing service relationship requires longer retention.

Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information against loss, unauthorised access, or disclosure. Our website is hosted on infrastructure with industry-standard encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

Your rights

Under POPIA, you have the right to: request access to the personal information we hold about you; request correction of inaccurate information; request deletion of your information; object to our processing of your information; and lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa if you believe your information has been mishandled.

Information Regulator contact details: inforeg@justice.gov.za / inforegulator.org.za

Contact us about your information

To exercise any of these rights, or ask a question about how your information is used, contact us at info@boreholepretoriaeast.co.za or 082 307 6988.
