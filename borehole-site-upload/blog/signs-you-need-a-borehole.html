7 Signs Your Pretoria East Home Needs a Borehole

Published 2026 · 4 min read

Municipal water supply in Pretoria isn't always reliable, and Pretoria East's larger gardens and estates put extra strain on tap water. Here are the clearest signs it's time to consider drilling your own borehole.

1. Frequent water outages or low pressure

If your suburb regularly experiences outages or pressure drops — especially in Faerie Glen, Wapadrand or Equestria during peak demand periods — a borehole removes your dependence on the municipal network entirely.

2. High water bills from garden irrigation

Large stands in Moreleta Park, Mooikloof and Woodhill often carry big irrigation costs. A borehole for garden and pool use alone can pay for itself within a few years.

3. Water restrictions during dry months

Municipal restrictions on garden watering hit hardest in spring and early summer. Borehole water isn't subject to these restrictions.

4. You're on a smallholding or larger property

Properties in Equestria and Mooikloof with paddocks, boarding facilities or large gardens need volumes of water that municipal supply struggles to justify cost-wise.

5. You're building or renovating

If you're already doing site work, drilling during construction is far less disruptive than doing it afterward.

6. You want backup water security

Load shedding affects municipal pump stations too. A borehole with its own pump and, ideally, backup power gives you water independence.

7. Your neighbours already have one

If several properties on your street already have working boreholes, that's a strong sign the local geology will support yours too.
