Borehole Drilling Cost in Pretoria East (2026 Guide)

Published 2026 · 5 min read

If you're a homeowner in Faerie Glen, Moreleta Park, Garsfontein or anywhere else in Pretoria East, the first question is almost always the same: how much does a borehole actually cost? Here's a realistic 2026 breakdown.

The short answer

A complete residential borehole — drilling, casing, pump installation, yield test and water quality test — typically costs between R40,000 and R140,000 in Pretoria East, with most homes landing between R65,000 and R100,000.

What drives the price

Depth. Pretoria East sits largely on the Quartzite Ridge, and most boreholes reach water at 60–90 metres. Drilling is priced per metre (roughly R300–R500/m), so depth is the single biggest cost driver.

Yield. Good sites in this part of Pretoria deliver 1,500–3,000 litres per hour. Lower-yield sites may need deeper drilling or a second borehole attempt, which affects total cost.

Casing and pump size. Larger households or properties with irrigation and pool top-up needs require bigger pumps and sometimes pressure tanks, adding to the installation cost.

Testing. A yield test (R1,500–R5,000) and water quality test (R1,500–R3,000) aren't optional extras — they protect you from installing an expensive pump on a borehole that can't support it.
