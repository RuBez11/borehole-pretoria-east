Borehole Drilling Areas — Pretoria East

We drill throughout Pretoria East. Jump to your suburb below for local geology notes and typical depths.

Borehole Drilling in Faerie Glen

Faerie Glen sits on the Quartzite Ridge, generally giving good yields of 1,500–3,000 L/hour at 60–90m depth. A popular choice for both estate and freestanding home boreholes.

Borehole Drilling in Moreleta Park

Moreleta Park's larger stands and estates make it one of our most active areas for full drill-to-tap installations, including irrigation-focused setups.

Borehole Drilling in Garsfontein

Garsfontein properties typically see similar quartzite-ridge yields to neighbouring Faerie Glen and Moreleta Park, with reliable water at moderate depths.

Borehole Drilling in Wapadrand

Wapadrand's rockier terrain sometimes requires deeper drilling, but yields are consistently strong once water-bearing fractures are reached.

Borehole Drilling in Waterkloof

Older, established Waterkloof properties often already have boreholes — we also handle repairs, re-drills and yield testing on existing systems.

Borehole Drilling in Silver Lakes

Silver Lakes estate homes commonly combine borehole water with irrigation and pool top-up systems — we size pumps accordingly.

Borehole Drilling in Woodhill

Woodhill Golf Estate and surrounds benefit from strong groundwater access, ideal for both household and garden irrigation demand.

Borehole Drilling in Equestria

Equestria's mix of residential and smallholding properties means we tailor packages from basic household supply to higher-yield agricultural use.

Borehole Drilling in Mooikloof

Mooikloof's larger estate stands are well suited to boreholes serving both the home and extensive gardens or paddocks.

Borehole Drilling in Menlyn

Menlyn's mixed residential and commercial properties often need boreholes for backup supply during municipal outages.

Borehole Drilling in Lynnwood

Lynnwood's established suburbs are a common area for both new boreholes and refurbishing older, disused ones.

Borehole Drilling in Brooklyn

Brooklyn's proximity to Pretoria's CBD doesn't affect groundwater access — yields remain solid at typical Pretoria East depths.

Borehole Drilling in Monument Park

Monument Park homeowners frequently combine a household borehole with garden irrigation for water security year-round.

Borehole Drilling in Sunnyside

Sunnyside's denser residential plots require careful site assessment to find optimal drill points on smaller stands.

Borehole Drilling in Arcadia

Arcadia properties, including guesthouses and small complexes, benefit from boreholes as a reliable backup water source.
